Ideally, I would love for such an opportunity to grow into a longer-term collaboration and potentially continued employment after completing my studies, and I am always happy to connect with companies interested in working together.

## Projects 
### Multiplayer Game for WisDome

Bachelor's Thesis Project | Unreal Engine 5 | C++ | Blueprints | WebSockets | Blender

Developed an interactive multiplayer game for the WisDome dome theatre as part of my Bachelor's thesis at Linköping University.

The project was carried out in a team of six students and was structured as a real-world development project, including project planning, teamwork, iterative development, testing and delivery to an external client. Alongside my development work, I also took on the role of Scrum Master.

The final prototype consisted of several multiplayer minigames where visitors could use their smartphones as controllers and interact with content displayed inside the dome.

My contributions included:

Unreal Engine development
Multiplayer communication using WebSockets
Gyroscope-based controls
Development of a web-based administration interface
3D environment creation
Blender modelling
Performance optimisation
Scrum Master responsibilities and team coordination
